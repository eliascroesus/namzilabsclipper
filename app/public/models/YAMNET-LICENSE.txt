yamnet.tflite

YAMNet, a sound event classifier trained on AudioSet (521 classes), from the
TensorFlow Model Garden (https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/audioset/yamnet),
as packaged for MediaPipe's audio classifier
(https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/audio_classifier/yamnet/float32/1/yamnet.tflite),
its label list in the file's metadata. It runs in the page on MediaPipe's
audio task runtime (@mediapipe/tasks-audio, Apache 2.0).
